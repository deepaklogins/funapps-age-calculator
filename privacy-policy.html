Privacy Policy

We do not collect any personal information. The App works entirely on your device. Dates you enter are processed locally and never transmitted to any server.

All data you enter is processed in your device's local memory during your session. We do not use databases, cloud storage, or any form of remote data persistence.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Sharing Feature

The App includes a "Share" feature that uses your device's native sharing functionality. We do not intercept, store, or have access to shared content.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages and collects no data from any user.
